# S-23.11: Corpus migration — behavioral-contracts files document class (46 files)

## Narrative

- **As a** state-manager/consistency-validator executing ADR-045's mechanical corpus migration
- **I want** to run the S-23.02 codemod across the 46 behavioral-contract files identified in
  ADR-045's corpus scan, converting live inline citations to other BCs/ADRs/Stories/VPs (within
  each BC's own Traceability section, Rationale prose, etc.) to stable-anchor form
- **So that** BC files — the source-of-truth artifacts POLICY 7 governs — no longer propagate
  version-pin churn to their own citations of sibling BCs, superseding ADRs, or anchor stories

## Context / Problem Statement

ADR-045 §Consequences, Negative/Trade-offs names 46 BC files in the corpus scan's live-pin count.
Unlike a citing document's reference TO a BC (handled in S-23.06/S-23.10/S-23.12/S-23.13), this
story handles a BC file's own citations OUT to other stable IDs — most commonly its Traceability
section's ADR cite (already partially governed by POLICY 19/TD-VSDD-091, per ADR-045 §Context,
"Policy 19 / TD-VSDD-091 demonstrates that narrowly-scoped elimination works") and any sibling-BC
or anchor-story cite in its Rationale/Context prose. A BC file's OWN `version:` frontmatter field
and its own BC-INDEX row are explicitly out of scope (S-23.05 AC-003 — ground truth, not a
citation).

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001
Running the codemod in `--dry-run` mode across all 46 BC files produces a diff report reviewed
before apply. (traces to ADR-045 §Migration Plan Phase A step 5; S-23.02 AC-006/AC-007)

### AC-002
Every BC Traceability-section ADR cite already in stable-anchor form under POLICY 19/TD-VSDD-091
(`ADR-NNN §Decision N`, no version) is left unchanged by the codemod (idempotence, S-23.02 AC-005)
— this story is a superset migration that must not disturb POLICY 19's already-compliant subset.
(traces to ADR-045 §Context, "Policy 19 / TD-VSDD-091 demonstrates that narrowly-scoped
elimination works"; S-23.02 AC-005)

### AC-003
Any BC Traceability-section ADR cite NOT yet in stable-anchor form (a residual pre-POLICY-19-era
or missed cite) is converted. (traces to ADR-045 §Policy Amendments Required, POLICY 19 row,
"scope extended to ALL load-bearing cross-reference positions")

### AC-004
Sibling-BC citations within a BC's Rationale/Context prose (e.g., "supersedes BC-1.03.016 v1.4")
are converted to stable-anchor form. (traces to ADR-045 §Decision, Tier 1, "Permitted forms")

### AC-005
The codemod does not modify a BC file's own `version:` frontmatter field, its own H1 title (POLICY
7 source of truth), or its `modified:`/`last_amended:`/`[Prior:]`/`## Changelog` sections. (traces
to S-23.05 AC-003; S-23.01 AC-001)

### AC-006
The migration is applied as exactly one atomic commit touching the 46 BC files. (traces to
ADR-045 §Migration Plan Phase A step 5; TD-VSDD-053)

### AC-007
Post-commit, S-23.03's guard and S-23.05's extended parity gate pass against all 46 migrated BC
files with zero findings. (traces to S-23.03 AC-001/AC-003; S-23.05 AC-001/AC-003)

### AC-008 (negative control)
Post-commit, a targeted grep for live-section inline version pins across all 46 BC files returns
zero matches. (traces to ADR-045 §Consequences Positive)
